A little note for you

Somewhere under
the northern lights

I have a little dream I keep coming back to — and it has you in it.

I want to see the aurora borealis with you.

Not by flying there, though. I’m imagining us taking the road instead — windows full of winter, our favourite songs playing, warm drinks in the cup holders, and nowhere to be in a hurry.

We’ll drive as far north as the road takes us, pull over somewhere quiet, look up at the sky, and wait for it to glow.

And if the lights don’t show, that’s okay. I’ll still be exactly where I want to be: on an adventure with you.

Would you take that road with me?

no planes · just us · one very long road
